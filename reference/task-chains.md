# Task chains

The second-order tasks. When the trigger on the left appears, the items on the
right are what actually has to happen. Most family failures live in rows 2
through 5 of these chains, not row 1.

## A birthday party invitation arrives

1. RSVP within 48 hours
2. Buy the gift, 5 days before
3. Wrap it, and confirm a card exists
4. Confirm who is doing drop-off and pickup, and the exact address
5. Block the calendar so nothing else lands on that window
6. If it is a drop-off party, confirm the host has a cell number for the parent
7. Note the family in `<data folder>/social-ledger.local.md` for reciprocity

## The child is invited to a sleepover or playdate

1. Confirm with the other parent by text, get the address and end time
2. Confirm allergies and house rules were communicated both directions
3. Pack: pajamas, toothbrush, comfort item, spare clothes
4. Set a pickup reminder 30 minutes before
5. Reciprocate within 6 weeks

## Booking a family vacation

1. Confirm the dates against the school calendar, both ends
2. Confirm no conflicting commitments for either parent
3. Book flights at the lead-time window, every seat together
4. Book lodging with free cancellation, then re-shop at 30 days
5. Check passport expiration against the 6-month rule, for everyone traveling
6. Arrange care for anything left behind: mail, plants, packages
7. Notify the building or the landlord if required
8. Book airport transport
9. Book any timed-entry or restaurant that needs advance booking
10. Build the packing list 5 days out
11. Confirm travel insurance and seat assignments 72 hours out

## A school form comes home

1. Read it the day it arrives, do not let it sit in the bag
2. Extract the deadline and any prerequisite (a physical, a payment, a signature
   from both parents)
3. If it needs an appointment, book that first, it is the long pole
4. Complete and return, and confirm receipt
5. Photograph it into the family file before it goes back

## A new medical appointment is booked

1. Add it to the shared calendar with the address, not just the time
2. Confirm who is taking the child and arrange coverage for the school pickup
3. Confirm insurance is accepted and whether a referral is needed
4. Prepare the questions to ask
5. At the appointment, book the next one before leaving
6. File the visit summary and update `<data folder>/health-log.local.md`
7. Submit the claim if out of network

## Camp or afterschool registration opens

1. Confirm the exact open date and time, and set an alarm for it
2. Confirm login credentials work the day before
3. Have the payment method ready
4. Register at open, do not browse first
5. Pay the deposit
6. Calendar the form and physical deadline, usually 6 weeks before
7. Buy the required gear from the packing list

## An anniversary or a partner's birthday approaches

Only run this chain when config lists a partner. Skip it otherwise.

1. Decide the shape of it 6 weeks out: dinner, trip, at home
2. Book the reservation or the travel
3. Book the sitter, 3 weeks minimum
4. Buy the gift, 2 weeks out
5. Get a card, this is the step that gets skipped
6. Block the whole evening on the calendar, not just the reservation

## The child's own birthday

Anchor to the child's birthdate from config, and count every step back from it.

1. Three months out: decide the shape of the party
2. Ten weeks out: book the venue, 8 to 10 weeks is the window
3. Four weeks out: send invitations
4. Three weeks out: order the cake, favors, and any custom items
5. Two weeks out: chase non-responders
6. One week out: confirm headcount with the venue
7. Separately: the gift from the parents, ordered 2 weeks out with shipping
   slack
8. After: thank-you notes within one week

## A growth spurt is noticed

1. Re-measure height, shoe, waist and update `<data folder>/sizes.local.md`
2. Audit the current season's clothes for what no longer fits
3. Buy replacements one size up
4. Check that shoes and outerwear specifically still fit, they fail first
5. Donate or store the outgrown set
6. Note the date, so the next spurt can be anticipated
