# Lead times

The core reference. Every obligation gets an action date computed by
subtracting the lead time from the deadline. Where a range is given, use the
earlier end, because being early costs nothing and being late costs money or a
seat.

Verify anything price-sensitive with a live search rather than trusting this
table blindly. Airline pricing behavior in particular shifts year to year.

## Travel

| Booking | Book by | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Domestic flight, off-peak | 6 to 8 weeks out | Bottom of the typical price curve |
| Domestic flight, peak or holiday | 3 to 4 months out | Thanksgiving, Christmas, spring break climb hard after this |
| International, off-peak | 3 to 4 months out | |
| International, peak or summer | 5 to 7 months out | Summer fares climb steeply inside 5 months |
| Award seats | 8 to 11 months out | Saver award inventory releases at schedule open and goes fast for seats together |
| Hotel with free cancellation | Book early, re-shop at 30 days | No downside; re-check for a drop |
| Rental car | Book early, re-shop monthly | Rates move both directions and cancellation is free |
| Passport renewal | 6 months before travel | Many countries require 6 months validity remaining |
| Kid passport (under 16) | 5 months, both parents must appear | Cannot be renewed by mail |
| Popular restaurant on vacation | Booking window open date, often 30 days | |
| Timed-entry attraction tickets | 4 to 8 weeks | |

**Party-size rule.** Award and cheap-fare inventory is per-seat. Two seats
together vanish before one does, three before two, four before three. Take the
party size from config and add roughly two weeks to the award booking window
for every seat past the first.

## School

| Obligation | Act by |
|---|---|
| Health forms and immunization records due | 8 weeks before, so the appointment fits |
| Re-enrollment contract and deposit | Day it opens; late fees and seat risk are real |
| Parent-teacher conference sign-up | Hour the slots open, good slots go same day |
| Field trip permission slip | Day it arrives; do not let it sit in the bag |
| Class party / volunteer signup | Within 48 hours of the ask |
| Tuition payment | 2 weeks before due |
| Neuropsych or specialist evaluation | 3 to 5 months; good evaluators book far out |
| District special education evaluation follow-up | Every 2 weeks once a referral is in |
| Next-year school application (if switching) | 12 months; deadlines often fall the autumn prior |

## Medical

| Appointment | Cadence | Book ahead |
|---|---|---|
| Pediatric well visit | Annual, near birthday | 8 weeks |
| Dentist cleaning | Every 6 months | 6 weeks |
| Vision screening | Annual once school-age | 4 weeks |
| Flu shot | Annual, Sept to Oct | 3 weeks |
| Specialist follow-up | Per doctor | Book the next one at the current visit |

Anchor the annual well visit to the child's birthday month, taken from config.
A birthday-anchored visit is easy to remember and usually clears the September
school-forms crunch. The dentist then falls in that month and six months later.

## Growth and clothing

Between ages 5 and 8 a child gains roughly 2 to 2.5 inches and half a shoe size
a year, but it comes in bursts, not evenly.

| Action | When |
|---|---|
| Measure height, weight, shoe, waist | Every 3 months, and at the annual well visit |
| Buy fall and winter clothes | Late July |
| Buy spring and summer clothes | Late February |
| Snow gear (boots, snow pants) | Late September, before the first cold snap sells it out |
| Rain boots and shell | Early March |
| New sneakers | Every 4 to 5 months in that age range |
| Swim gear | Early May |

Buy the next size up when the current size is within a half size of tight. A
size that fits perfectly in August will not fit in November.

## Social

| Obligation | Act by |
|---|---|
| RSVP to a kid's party | 48 hours of receiving it, always |
| Gift for that party | 5 days before |
| Booking a venue for the child's own party | 8 to 10 weeks; kid venues book out, more in dense markets |
| Invitations for that party | 4 weeks before |
| Anniversary plan | 6 weeks out for a restaurant worth going to |
| Partner's birthday gift | Start 4 weeks out, not the week of |
| Date night reservation | 2 to 3 weeks for anything good on a weekend |
| Sitter for a specific date | 3 weeks; longer for a holiday or New Year's Eve |
| Thank-you note | Within 1 week of the gift or the hosting |

## Camp, afterschool, seasonal

| Obligation | Act by |
|---|---|
| Summer camp registration | Opens Jan to Feb in most markets; popular sessions sell out in days. Verify the real open date |
| Camp deposit | At registration |
| Camp forms and physical | 6 weeks before the session |
| Afterschool / enrichment enrollment | Opens Aug for fall, Dec for spring |
| Holiday travel dates locked | September, before fares move |
| Winter holiday gift shopping | Start Nov 1, finish before Dec 10 |
| Seasonal closet swap | Late September and late March |
| Window AC units in / out | May and October, apartments only, check building rules |
| Gutters, exterior, yard | Spring and fall, houses only |
