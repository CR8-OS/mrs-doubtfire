# Special education and evaluation log

The contemporaneous record of the district's special education process for your
child: referral, consent, evaluation, eligibility, and services. This record
matters if the timeline is ever disputed, so every contact gets logged the day
it happens.

One file per child if more than one is in the process.

Verify the current statutory timelines with a live source rather than from
memory. They are set by federal law and by your state, and they differ by
state. Under IDEA the district generally has 60 days from written parental
consent to complete the evaluation, but check which 60 days your state means.

## Standing rules

- Follow up every two weeks. Silence is not progress.
- After every phone call, send a short confirming email restating what was
  agreed. Draft it, the parent sends it.
- Private evaluations often book 3 to 5 months out. That is the long pole.
- If the district misses a statutory deadline, that is the point to bring in an
  advocate or an attorney.
