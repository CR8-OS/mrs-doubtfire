## Seasonal swap checklist

Two swaps a year. Buy before the season, not during it, because the sizes that
sell out first are the ones you need.

**Into the cold season**
- [ ] Clothes audit and buy up, outerwear and shoes first
- [ ] Snow boots and snow pants, before they sell out
- [ ] Heavy bedding out
- [ ] Humidifier
- [ ] Cooling equipment stored

**Into the warm season**
- [ ] Clothes audit and buy up
- [ ] Rain boots and shell
- [ ] Light bedding
- [ ] Cooling equipment in and serviced
